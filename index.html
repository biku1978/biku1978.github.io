About Me

Connecting evidence, people and development results.

I am a development professional with over eleven years of progressive experience in programme management, technical advisory and development cooperation, primarily within Nepal's health sector.

My professional work brings together results-based monitoring, strategic communication, programme coordination and knowledge management. I contribute to strengthening the processes and institutional capacities that enable development initiatives to achieve meaningful and measurable results.

In my current advisory role with GIZ, I support health sector development initiatives through results monitoring, programme performance management, communication and collaboration with government institutions and development stakeholders.

My experience involves working with government counterparts, development partners, civil society organisations and local institutions. I value practical approaches that connect programme objectives with institutional ownership, accountability and sustainable outcomes.

I believe that development is not simply about implementing activities. It is about understanding change, learning from evidence, building partnerships and improving the systems that serve people.

My Professional Perspective

Effective development requires more than resources and activities. It requires strong institutions, reliable evidence, meaningful participation and a clear understanding of results.

I am particularly interested in how monitoring, communication and collaboration can work together to improve development effectiveness and strengthen public systems.

"Measure what matters. Communicate what changes. Strengthen what lasts."
